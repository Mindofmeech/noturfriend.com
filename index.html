Questions

FAQ

How do I book?

Choose a service in Book and pick a time on Ashlee's calendar. A $25 deposit is applied to your service total. Please cancel or reschedule at least 2 days before the start of your appointment. Deposits are kept for late cancellations and no-shows. You can still DM @noturfriend.hair with the service you want and a photo of your hair goals.

Is there a deposit?

A $25 deposit is applied to your service total. Please cancel or reschedule at least 2 days before the start of your appointment. Deposits are kept for late cancellations and no-shows.

Are consultations free?

Consultations are always free.

How is an extension install priced?

Extension install is priced after a free consult. The appointment runs 3h 30m.

What is the cancellation policy?

A $25 deposit is applied to your service total. Please cancel or reschedule at least 2 days before the start of your appointment. Deposits are kept for late cancellations and no-shows.

What if I am running late?

There's a 15-minute grace period. Arrivals after that may require rescheduling.

What are the hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Sunday is closed.

Where is the studio?

Suite 110, Sterling Professional Center, 2909 Ranch Road 620, Austin, Texas.

What does Ashlee specialize in?

Blonde specialist, curly hair, color correction, balayage and highlights, bridal and events, extensions, natural hair, Asian hair, and allergy-safe color.

How long has Ashlee been doing hair?

Nearly 6 years.
